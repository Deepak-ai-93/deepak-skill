# Repurposing Hub Plan

**Source:** The 5-Page Pricing Fix — founder's blog post on SaaS pricing mistakes
**The ONE story:** Most founders lose revenue on their pricing page not with pricing, but with page structure — one afternoon of restructuring tripled revenue per visitor in 30 days
**Audience:** SaaS founders & indie hackers · **Goal:** grow followers on X + LinkedIn and newsletter subscribers · **Primary CTA:** get the pricing teardown

Same story, native retelling on every platform. Each piece opens a different loop, escalates in its platform's native format, pays off with the same aha, and closes with its own CTA. No copy-paste reposts — every hook is unique (script-verified).

## Angle bank (extracted from the source)

- **Hook / entry points:** Your pricing page is losing money before anyone sees the price · The pricing fix that tripled revenue per visitor in 30 days
- **Key beats:** 4 mistakes: features-not-outcomes, hidden price, one plan, watching signups not funnel · the one-page fix · 3× revenue per visitor, -40% support time
- **The aha:** one page, one price, one outcome — the price does the qualifying
- **Quotable line:** If I have to call you to see a price, I'm out.
- **Proof:** 3× revenue per visitor in 30 days · 0.4% → 1.2% pricing-page conversion
