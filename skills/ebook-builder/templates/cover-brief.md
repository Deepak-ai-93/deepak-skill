# Cover brief — reference for ebook-builder

The cover is the download decision. One glance, one idea, one emotion.

## The cover rules (non-negotiable)

1. **ONE idea.** The title is the promise: "The 5 Pricing Mistakes That Cost You Revenue" — not "A Guide to Better SaaS Pricing in Today's Fast-Paced World".
2. **Title ≤ 6 words.** If it doesn't fit at thumbnail size, it doesn't fit at all.
3. **Readable at thumbnail.** Cover text must survive 200×280px: high contrast (scrim/panel behind type), one type pairing, no thin fonts on busy imagery.
4. **An emotion.** The cover either threatens (loss aversion — "the mistake that costs you $12k"), promises (result — "triple revenue per visitor"), or teases (curiosity — "the button nobody clicks").
5. **Subtitle ≤ 12 words** only if it strengthens the promise; otherwise omit.
6. **A visual.** Mode 1: the designed cover (palette block + type, or pattern/scene-frame/duotone per the picker). Mode 2: a photoreal scene that matches the emotion — real person/moment/prop, no stock clichés.

## The brief template (fill per ebook)

```
Cover title (≤ 6 words): {title}
Subtitle (≤ 12 words, optional): {subtitle}
Emotion / hook formula: {loss-aversion | result | curiosity}
Cover style: {full-bleed | solid | split | pattern-geometric | scene-frame | duotone}
Layout + palette + accent: {layout} · {palette} · {accent hex}
Motif family + mood + texture: {motif} · {mood} · {texture}  # repeat on every page (design picker)
SCENE (Mode 2 — photoreal): {who, where, when, prop, emotion — e.g. "a founder's hands over a laptop at 11pm, one warm lamp, a pricing page on screen"}
Text overlay: "{title}" — {placement: bottom 45% over a dark scrim, white, heavy sans}
```

## Anti-clichés

No gold bars · no hand-on-chin thinking pose · no "$" raining from the sky · no rocket ships · no generic "strategy" icons as the main visual. The scene IS the promise.
