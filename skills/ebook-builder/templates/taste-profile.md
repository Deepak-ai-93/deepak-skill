# Taste capture — find the author's voice before writing one word

**Purpose:** the ebook's copy (cover title, headlines, body, CTA) must read like the **author wrote it** — not like generic AI copy. Capture the voice at **Stage 0** in ≤ 3 questions (or one pasted sample), write the result into `ebook-memory.md` §2, and apply it to every word in the deck.

## Ask the author (pick the 2–3 that fit — never interrogate)

1. "Paste one paragraph you wrote that you love (an email, a thread, a blog post). I'll copy your rhythm, not your topic."
2. "What's your tone when you teach? — warm + direct / sharp + confident / playful + punchy / calm + measured / other"
3. "Short punchy sentences or longer flowing ones?"
4. "Any words you hate in your niche (overused, cringe)? I'll ban them in the ebook — the audit will fail the deck on them."
5. "How do you open things — with a story, a stat, a question, or straight to the point?"

## Fast path — the author won't answer

Extract the voice from what they DID give you: the request itself, their brand, their niche, their audience.
- Defaults: direct + confident · short declarative sentences · heavy "you" · zero fluff · one specific number per claim.

## Applying the taste (the rail)

- **Cover title:** their way of promising — punchy, ≤ 6 words, zero buzzwords.
- **Headlines:** ≤ 8 words, in their voice.
- **Body:** reads like they typed it — their rhythm, their specifics, one hard number per claim.
- **CTA:** their actual ask, not "click here to unlock".
- **Test:** read the deck out loud. If a line could have been written by any AI for any niche, rewrite it in their voice.
