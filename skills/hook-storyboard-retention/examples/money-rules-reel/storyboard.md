# Worked example — "3 Money Rules Nobody Told You" (30s reel)

> A complete worked output of the hook-storyboard-retention skill for one reel:
> trend call → hook pack → retention-curve diagnosis → 30s storyboard → audit.
> This is the shape of the deliverable (`storyboard.md`) the skill produces.

## 1. Trend call (Step 0)
- **Niche:** personal finance · **Signal:** "money rules" is a top-of-day r/personalfinance topic; comment sections show people *arguing* about rule #3 (the hook).
- **Angle:** 3 contrarian money rules with receipts — the argument IS the retention.

## 2. Hook pack (Step 1 — pick the winner)
| # | Formula | Hook (≤ 12 words) | Why it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contrarian** | "Stop paying your credit card bill on time." | Pattern interrupt — safe, untrue, argues with the viewer |
| 2 | **Curiosity gap** | "The money rule banks don't want you to know." | Vague + threatening to the powerful |
| 3 | **Results** | "I followed 3 money rules for 90 days. Here's what broke." | Specific, timed, outcome promised |

**Winner:** #1 — opens a loop (why would I stop?) and sets up the payoff (pay early ≠ pay on time).

## 3. Retention-curve diagnosis (Step 2 — before writing)
- **Hump at 0–3s:** hook must *visually* flip (beat 1 = bold claim on screen, not talking head).
- **Loop 1 closed at 10s:** rule 1 payoff lands → open loop 2 ("rule 2 is worse").
- **Loop 3 at 25s closes the master loop** → the "you" line pays off the opening claim.

## 4. Storyboard beat sheet (30s, Step 3)
| Time | Visual | Text on screen | VO (Kokoro, `am_michael`) | Beat role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Card flip: "STOP paying on time" | STOP paying your card on time. | "Stop paying your credit card bill on time." | Hook — pattern interrupt |
| 3–10s | Receipt animation, interest number grows | 28.7% APR | "The bank prices the statement balance. Pay 2 days early and you shrink the interest window." | Rule 1 payoff + receipts |
| 10–16s | "Rule 2 is worse" card | RULE 2 IS WORSE | "Rule 2: never use your credit limit as a budget." | Loop 2 opens |
| 16–25s | Two-column compare: credit vs debit | 2× spending | "People who swipe credit spend ~2×. Close the card in a drawer." | Rule 2 payoff + escalation |
| 25–30s | The "you" line, then CTA card | YOU KNEW #3 | "You knew rule 3. You just didn't want to say it." | Master loop closes → comment bait |

## 5. Audit (Step 5 — excerpt)
- `audit-storyboard.mjs` → 0 FAIL: spine beats present (hook → 2 open loops → payoff → loop ending), fluff rule held (every beat raises a question or pays one off), char caps on all text lines.
- **storyboard-auditor verdict:** 41/50 → **PASS** (worth producing).
