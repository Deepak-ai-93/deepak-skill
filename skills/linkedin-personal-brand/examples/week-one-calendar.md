# Week 1 Calendar — example (founder of a SaaS onboarding tool)

> Voice profile: "Maya Chen — Product Lead, Northbeam. Helps SaaS founders stop losing signups on day 1. Writes like she talks: short sentences, numbers, no buzzwords. Never uses 'leverage', 'synergy', or 'passionate'."

| Day | Role | Hook (first 2 lines) | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue | Story | "My best client fired us in month 3. Best thing that happened to our product." | "What's a firing that ended up helping you? Comment below." |
| Wed | Teaching | "We cut our own signup time from 40 minutes to 6. The change cost 1 line of code." | "Repost for the founder who's losing signups this month." |
| Thu | Contrarian | "Everyone tells you to build a better onboarding. Your onboarding isn't the problem — your pricing page is." | "Agree or not — tell me where you think the leak is." |
| Sat | Win/receipt | "41 dead-ends found in our funnel last month. We fixed the top 3 and completion went up 11%." | "The teardown is linked below if you want the checklist." |

## Week 1 rationale
- **Role variety:** story → teaching → contrarian → win — four different jobs, no repetition.
- **Proof in every post:** each has a number or a story (month 3, 40→6 minutes, 41 dead-ends, 11%).
- **One CTA each**, all mapped to the month's goal (DM conversations + profile visits).
- **Cadence:** 4 posts, Tue–Sat; comment strategy (see `engagement.md`) fills the other days.
- **Week 2 plan:** check analytics — whichever role got the most comments gets posted twice next week.
