# Post Formulas + Structure — LinkedIn Personal Brand

## The 5 post roles (vary these in the calendar — never five of the same)

| Role | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Story** | A real event with a before/after — the most shareable | Wins, failures, client stories |
| **Teaching** | One specific how-to with a proof element | Frameworks, processes, lessons |
| **Contrarian** | A defensible take against common advice | Positioning, differentiation |
| **Win/receipt** | A result with numbers + how it happened | Credibility, proof |
| **Question** | A genuinely useful question the audience answers | Engagement, research |

## The universal post skeleton

```
[HOOK — 2 lines max: the specific claim, story beat, or number]
[blank line]
[CONTEXT — who this is for, why it matters, in your voice]
[THE MEAT — the story/teaching, with ONE proof element: number, name, screenshot, outcome]
[blank line]
[PAYOFF — the lesson or takeaway, specific not generic]
[ONE CTA — comment / repost / DM / follow link]
[1–3 relevant hashtags max]
```

## Hook formulas (first 2 lines)

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Result-first | "I cut our agency's reporting time 61% in 8 weeks." |
| Failure-first | "My best client fired us in month 3. Best thing that happened." |
| Contrarian | "Everyone tells founders to niche down. I think they're half right." |
| Specific number | "$40k on a retainer that did nothing." |
| Story tease | "A founder DM'd me this morning with a pricing page I can't unsee." |
| Pattern interrupt | "I don't post tips. I post receipts." |

**Rules:** hook must be TRUE and matched by the body (LinkedIn readers + the algorithm punish bait) · 2 lines max · no "Agree? 👇" · no "Who else feels this?"

## CTA rules
- ONE per post. "Comment your take" / "Repost for someone pricing this month" / "DM 'template' and I'll send it" — specific beats vague.
- Every post should be *shareable*, but only the calendar-marked posts ask for the repost.

## Anti-fluff blocklist (never in posts or bio)
`passionate` · `guru` · `ninja` · `thought leader` · `synergy` · `game-changer` · `elevate` · `supercharge` · `unlock` · `journey` · `leverage` · `in today's fast-paced world` · `agreed?` · `who else feels` · `like this post if` · hashtag walls (keep ≤ 3, relevant).

## Cadence rules
- 2–5 posts/week; 3 is the sweet spot for most founders.
- Vary roles (max 2 of the same role per week).
- Best engagement windows (general): Tue–Thu, 8–10am in the audience's timezone — check your own analytics and adjust.
- Comment 30–60 min/day on target accounts (add ONE insight, never "Great post!").
