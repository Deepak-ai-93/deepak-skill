# Voice Capture — LinkedIn Personal Brand

Walk the user through this BEFORE writing anything. The output becomes `voice-profile.md`. Each answer: ask them to answer in their own words — their exact phrasing IS the voice profile.

> Rule: if they type one line, ask one follow-up ("tell me more — how did that actually go?"). Never fill their answers with your own words.

---

## 1. Positioning (the foundation)

1. What do you do — in one sentence a 12-year-old would get?
2. Who do you help, and what's the specific result they get? (Not "businesses" — "freelance developers who waste 5 hours/week on client reporting".)
3. What's the ONE thing you're known for / want to be known for?
4. Who are your ideal followers/clients — what do they struggle with right now?

## 2. Proof arsenal (this becomes your content)

5. Your 3 biggest career wins — with numbers if possible ("cut churn 18% in a quarter", "shipped to 40k users").
6. Your 2 best failures — what happened, what it cost, what you'd do differently. (Failures are the most-clicked content — be specific.)
7. A client/customer story with a real outcome (name + company or anonymized).
8. What data/tools do you have access to that most people in your niche don't?

## 3. Voice (how you actually talk)

9. What do you believe that your industry disagrees with? (Contrarian angles live here.)
10. Pick 2–3 posts by other people you'd genuinely repost — what drew you in?
11. Share 2–3 real emails / posts / notes you've written recently (your phrasing is gold).
12. Words you'd NEVER use (buzzwords you hate: "synergy", "thought leader", "guru", "passionate about"...).

## 4. Topic pillars (repeatable content)

13. What 3–4 topics could you post about forever without running out?
14. What questions do clients/customers ask you repeatedly? (Each is a post.)
15. What's the outcome you want LinkedIn to drive? (Newsletter signups / DM conversations / inbound clients / speaking?)

---

## Turning answers into `voice-profile.md`

| Section | Source |
|---|---|
| Positioning statement | Q1–Q4, distilled into one paragraph |
| Tone rules (do / never) | Q9–Q12 — quote their actual phrases |
| Topic pillars (3–4) | Q13–Q14 |
| Proof arsenal | Q5–Q8 (numbers, stories, screenshots available) |
| CTA goal | Q15 |
| Example hooks | Q9–Q10, written in their voice |
