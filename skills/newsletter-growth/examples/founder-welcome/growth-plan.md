# Growth plan — "Pricing with receipts" (weekly, SaaS founders)

## Welcome sequence (live now)

1. **Welcome + origin** — "Every Tuesday: one pricing story with the numbers. No fluff." Set the cadence.
2. **Best of** — this issue ("The button that cost us $12,400") as the proof.
3. **How to read me** — reply with pricing questions; I answer the top 3 each month; unsubscribe link up top (trust).
4. **Referral ask (first)** — "Forward this to one founder pricing by feel."
5. **The personal stake** — why I write this: I lost $12,400 so you don't have to.

## Referral loop

- Growth plug in every issue (one line, bottom): "Know a founder who needs this? Forward it."
- Referral reward at 500 subs: "1 forward = the pricing teardown checklist."
- Thank public referrers with a shoutout in the next issue.
- Weekly measure: new subscribers from forwards (target ≥ 0.5 per 100 opens).

## Cross-promos (month 1)

- Swap with 2 newsletters: a SaaS-pricing one (same angle) + a founder-tools one (adjacent audience).
- Guest post on one bigger founder newsletter — bio line drives signups.
- Every issue → X posts (`x-growth`, zero hashtags) + LinkedIn post, link in bio.
