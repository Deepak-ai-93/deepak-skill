# Growth tactics — reference for newsletter-growth

The issue gets the opens; the plan compounds them into subscribers. Every newsletter pack ships a `growth-plan.md` built from these tactics.

## The welcome sequence (first 5 emails — non-negotiable)

| # | Email | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome + the origin story** | Confirm the subscription, tell the ONE-sentence promise of the newsletter, set the cadence ("every Tuesday"). |
| 2 | **The best of / proof issue** | Your single best previous issue — the one that made subscribers forward. |
| 3 | **The "how to read me" issue** | What the reader gets each week, what to reply with, how to unsubscribe (trust). |
| 4 | **The referral ask (first)** | The growth plug with a reason: "forward this to one founder — here's what they'll get." |
| 5 | **The personal stake** | Why you write this, what you're trying to build. The most-forwarded email type. |

Rules: one ask per email · never send the referral ask twice in a row · every email links back to the archive.

## The referral loop (the compounding engine)

1. **The plug lives in every issue** (bottom, one line): "Know a founder who needs this? Forward it." No begging, no points system needed at first.
2. **Referral rewards (once > 500 subscribers):** a free template/checklist after 1 referral, a not-so-secret bonus after 3. Beehiiv/Substack both support native referral tracking.
3. **Thank public referrers** — a "forwarded by" shoutout in the next issue turns one forward into a habit.
4. **Measure the loop weekly:** forwards/subscriber per issue. Healthy = ≥ 0.5 new subscribers per 100 opens from forwarding alone.

## Cross-promos (the fastest early growth)

- **Swap with 2–3 same-audience newsletters** (different niche angle): "issue swap" — each recommends the other in one issue. Aim for 1–2 swaps/month at < 1,000 subscribers; 2–4/month after.
- **Guest-write once a month** on a bigger newsletter in the niche — one bio line is worth 10 issues of solo writing.
- **Repurpose every issue** into X posts (`x-growth`, zero hashtags) + a LinkedIn post — the post drives the link, the link drives signups.

## Analytics review (day-7/14/30 loop)

| Metric | Healthy signal | Fix if weak |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 35–50% (editorial) | Subject formula is wrong or the promise overpromises |
| Click rate | > 3% | CTA isn't clear, or value bullets lack links |
| Reply rate | > 0.3% | Ask a question in the One ask; write in a real voice |
| New subscribers/issue | Positive trend | Referral plug + cross-promo cadence |
| Unsubscribe rate | < 0.5%/issue | Promise drift — the issue didn't match the subject |

Review every 7 days for the first month, then monthly. Kill what doesn't compound; double what does.
