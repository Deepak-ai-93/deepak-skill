# Ads Compliance Checklist — AI-generated creatives (verified as of: <LAUNCH DATE>)

> **Re-check before every launch.** Meta and Google update ad policies continuously. This checklist reflects the 2026 rules researched for this skill — verify against the live policy pages on your launch date.

## Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

- [ ] **Synthetic media / AI labeling** — AI-generated content that could be mistaken for real events, people or products follows Meta's synthetic-media rules (labeling where required). Political/social-issue ads with AI media get mandatory labels.
- [ ] **No AI misrepresentation** — AI assets must not mislead about product function, quality, or origin. The ad shows the actual product; AI renders its real shape/color/label.
- [ ] **Brand Memory / brand consistency** — AI-generated variants keep the brand's tone, colors and messaging on-identity (Meta's Brand Memory tool is allowed for this).
- [ ] **Real-person likeness** — using a real person's face or voice (AI or real) in an ad requires their consent (and an authorization form where Meta requires it).
- [ ] **Prohibited claims** — no exaggerated or unsubstantiated claims; health/financial claims need evidence.
- [ ] **Landing page match** — the destination page matches the ad's promise and works on mobile.

## Google Ads

- [ ] **AI label settings** — campaigns using AI-generated or AI-edited assets have the **AI label** enabled in Google Ads / DV360 / Merchant Center (mandatory for EU/India/NY compliance).
- [ ] **No deceptive deepfakes** — AI alterations of real people must be verifiable and non-deceptive.
- [ ] **Text Guidelines locked** — Google's Text Guidelines settings are configured to keep AI-written ad copy on-tone and on-brand.
- [ ] **Asset specs within limits** — images ≤5 MB (static ≤150 KB); video 1080p H.264/AAC; key elements inside the center 80% safe zone.
- [ ] **Prohibited content** — no misleading claims, no "guaranteed results" language, no regulated claims without certification.

## Universal (both platforms)

- [ ] Claims are substantiated (one source or spec per claim).
- [ ] No "guaranteed earnings/results", no fake urgency, no fake scarcity.
- [ ] CTA matches the landing page action (Shop Now → product page, not a generic homepage).
- [ ] Privacy/compliance: tracking consent signals handled (CAPI, consent mode); ad account business info accurate.
- [ ] Local law: age-gating for age-restricted products; regional ad disclosures applied.

## Quick test before launch

1. Open the ad on a phone: is the hook legible in the first 2 seconds without sound?
2. Does the landing page load fast and match the ad's promise?
3. Would a regulator object to any claim? If yes, rewrite it.
4. Is every AI-generated asset labeled where the platform requires it?
