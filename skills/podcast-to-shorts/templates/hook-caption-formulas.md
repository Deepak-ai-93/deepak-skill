# Hook + Caption Formulas — podcast-to-shorts

Use when writing the 2-second hook and platform captions for each scored clip.

---

## The 2-second hook (on-screen opener of the clip)

| Formula | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Bold claim | State a strong, arguable truth | "This is why your SaaS dies." |
| Specific number | Lead with the receipt | "$40k on a retainer that did nothing." |
| Story tease | Start inside the story | "My client quit in week two." |
| Pattern interrupt | Attack the common advice | "Everyone says niche down. They're wrong." |
| Question | Force self-reference | "Why do you keep buying tools you never open?" |
| Result-first | Lead with the outcome | "We cut onboarding from 40 to 6 minutes." |

**Rules:**
- The hook must match what's actually in the clip — clickbait mismatch = shame + lost trust.
- Never "So in this episode we talked about…" — start mid-moment, not meta.
- The hook line usually IS a line from the transcript — reuse it verbatim.

## Caption per platform

| Platform | Length | Style | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 100–220 chars | Punchy, one idea, emojis OK | "Follow for part 2" / "Full episode linked" |
| Reels | 100–220 chars | Same, conversation tone | "Save this / Full episode in bio" |
| Shorts | 300–400 chars | A little more room for context | "Full episode on the channel" |

Rules: hook first · no hashtag walls (1–3 max) · quote the exact clip line · one CTA · never "link in bio" without actually linking.

## Caption template

```
{THE CLIP'S LINE — the proof}
→ {why it matters, one sentence}
{CTA}
```

Example:
```
"Everyone says niche down. They're wrong."
We went from generic agency → "invoices for freelancers" and cut our ad cost 61% in 8 weeks. Full breakdown in the episode.
Follow for part 2 — the pricing page teardown.
```
