# Positioning — ClipDeck

## One-liner
ClipDeck gets design teams unstuck — feedback in videos, not meetings.

## Messaging hierarchy
- Audience: design-team leads at 10–60 person product companies who approve client or internal work
- Problem: feedback lives in long comment threads and meetings, so revisions stretch from days into weeks
- Promise: designers see the feedback and ship the fix — a revision cycle that fits in one afternoon
- Proof: teams that switch cut their feedback loop from 5 days to 6 hours

## Proof points
- Cut design-team feedback loops from 5 days to 6 hours for 40+ teams in the first quarter
- Studio North approved 12 revisions in a single afternoon after switching from thread comments
- Teams that switch hold 31% fewer status meetings in month one
- Average reviewer records a clip in 3 minutes — no account setup beyond an email link

## Taglines
- Feedback in videos, not meetings.
- Stop reading comments. Start watching them.
- Your revisions, approved by Friday.

## Voice guide
- Tone: direct + plain, zero agency-speak — talks like a PM who has lived the thread pain
- Rhythm: short declarative sentences; one hard number per claim
- Pet phrases (use sparingly): "the thread pain", "approved by Friday"
- **Banned words** (the audit FAILs the positioning on these):
  - synergy
  - disrupt
  - seamless
  - ninja
