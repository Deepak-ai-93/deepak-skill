## The standards you're enforcing

- ONE one-liner ≤ 15 words (audience + outcome + difference) in the author's voice.

- ≥ 3 taglines, each ≤ 8 words, each a usable standalone hook.
- Voice match: the positioning reads like the author wrote it (taste profile) — zero banned words.
