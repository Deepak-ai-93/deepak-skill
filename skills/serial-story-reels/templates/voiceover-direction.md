# Voiceover Direction — episodic series (serial-story-reels)

Two paths, chosen per series (or per episode):

1. **Native Veo audio** — dialogue is written INTO the scene prompt (`Dialogue: she says, tense, "..."`). Veo 3.1 generates the voice from the line + delivery tone. Zero extra work, voice is baked into the video.
2. **Kokoro post-production** — the `voiceover.md` line sheet feeds `voice-sfx-audio` (Kokoro TTS + SFX + mix to -14 LUFS). Use this when you want full control of the voice, a celebrity/character voice, or a specific accent the native audio can't hit.

The line sheet (`episode-prompts.mjs --vo voiceover.md`) is written in BOTH cases — it's the canonical record of every line + delivery.

---

## Writing dialogue for native Veo audio

Format inside the scene prompt:
```
Dialogue: {speaker} says, {delivery tone}, "{exact words}"
```
- **Label it `Dialogue:`** so the model treats it as audio, not visual description.
- **Exact words in quotes.** Veo speaks what's written — punctuation is the performance (periods, dashes, ellipses).
- **Delivery tone is mandatory** — one or two words, concrete: `breathless and desperate`, `low and calm`, `cold, clipped`, `warm and teasing`.

## Delivery vocabulary (pick per line)

| Tone bucket | Words |
|---|---|
| **Urgent** | breathless · desperate · urgent · panicked · shouted · strained · winded |
| **Calm** | low and calm · measured · warm · soft · intimate · resigned |
| **Cold** | cold and clipped · flat · clinical · deadpan · icy · monotone |
| **Bold** | punchy · cocky · confident · defiant · triumphant · teasing |
| **Fragile** | trembling · small voice · barely a whisper · cracking · hesitant |
| **Genre** | comic: booming announcer · love: breathy and intimate · thriller: whispered and paranoid · fantasy: resonant and formal · action: commanding through exertion |

## The voice-anchor trick (cross-episode voice consistency)

Veo's native audio may drift slightly between episodes. Counter:
1. In **Episode 1**, generate each character's FIRST speaking scene and extract the clean dialogue audio.
2. Save it as that character's **voice reference**.
3. For Episodes 2+, re-feed the reference audio with the new line (matches Veo's native audio anchoring, and Kokoro's voice-cloning path uses the same saved clip).

This is the audio twin of the character reference-image system — lock the voice once, reuse forever.

## Voiceover sheet format (generated by episode-prompts.mjs)

```
# Voiceover Sheet — "Neon Hearts"
**Mode:** native Veo dialogue (paste into the scene prompt) OR Kokoro post-production.

## Episode e1 — The First Beat
- **s1:** "Not today. Not this package." — *delivery: breathless*
- **s2:** "Not today!" — *delivery: shouting over the commotion*
- **s4:** "That's 'The Heart Thief' #1. My pages. My stolen pages." — *delivery: whispering, frozen*

> Voice anchor: save the clean dialogue from each character's FIRST scene as their voice reference for all later episodes.
```

The sheet is generated by `episode-prompts.mjs` — it splits each Veo dialogue line into **speech** (the quoted words only, ready for TTS) + **delivery** (the tone, extracted from the framing or the scene's `vo` field). No nested quotes, no framing words like "she says" in the spoken line.

## Rules

- Every speaking scene appears in the sheet (a scene with `dialogue` OR `vo` gets a row).
- Every line has a delivery direction — a bare quote with no tone is a FAIL.
- SFX and ambient are labeled (`SFX:` / `Ambient:`) in the prompt — the sheet covers speech only.
- Post: mix VO + music to **-14 LUFS** (voice-sfx-audio), add on-screen captions for the mute-scroll viewer.
