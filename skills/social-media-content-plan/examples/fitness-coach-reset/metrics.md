# Metrics + Review Loop — what the data decides

Track the 4 signals that correlate with compounding reach — not vanity
metrics. Log them after every post (2 minutes), review on the checkpoints,
and let the numbers change the calendar.

## The 4 compounding signals (track every post)

1. **Dwell / watch time** — Reels: average watch time + watch-through rate.
   LinkedIn: does the post hold 5+ seconds? X: read time is implied by reply
   velocity — treat replies as the proxy.
2. **Completion** — Reels/Shorts completed vs started; where the biggest drop
   is (that's the retention problem to fix in the next post).
3. **Save / share rate** — saves + shares per reach. Save-bait content should
   out-rate like-bait every time; if it doesn't, the hook/caption promise is
   mismatched.
4. **Follower conversion** — followers gained per post reach. This is the
   model's "does this creator deserve a wider test?" signal.

Log format (one line per post, in a notes file or spreadsheet):

```
2026-08-17 ig reel: 412 reach · 58% watched ≥50% · 21 saves · 3 DM shares · 4 follows · 12 comments in first hour
```

## Review checkpoints (calendar marks these days)

### Day 7 — "is the cluster working?"
- Which pillar + format won? Double its share in next week's slots.
- Which hook pattern won? Feed more of that pattern into the bank.
- First-hour velocity: are comments/replies happening? If not, the protocol
  isn't being run — fix the schedule, not the content.

### Day 14 — "sprint done, re-test begun"
- Same as Day 7, plus: any platform with zero tier-expansion (no new
  audience reach) gets its cadence halved and the budget moved to the winner.
- Kill one format that underperformed both weeks.

### Day 21 — "scale the winners"
- Top 3 posts of the sprint → plan 3 remakes with a new angle (never a clone).
- Double down on the winning pillar: it should now be ≥ 60% of the week.

### Day 30 — "the reset verdict"
- Compare Day 1–30: reach trend, follower growth, save/share trend.
- **Expectation setting:** the sprint's goal is re-testing (views in tiers:
  100 → 1K → 10K). If reach is up but still small, the model is re-learning —
  month two is where the compounding starts. If reach is flat everywhere, the
  niche is too broad or the content is failing completion — that's a content
  problem, not an algorithm problem.

## Kill / double-down rules

| Signal | Double down | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Save/share > 1% of reach | More save-bait utility content | Content with no saves |
| Completion > 70% | More of that format/hook | Reels with big early drops |
| First-hour comments > 10 | More open-loop endings | Posts with zero comments |
| Follower conversion > 0.5% | More of that pillar | Viral-adjacent posts that don't convert |
