# Content Pillars + Hook Bank — Dan (fitness coach)

Four narrow pillars, shares sum to 100%. Every post in the 30-day calendar
belongs to exactly one of these — that's what lets the algorithm build a
single stable embedding for "fat loss for busy professionals".

| Pillar | Share | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Transformation receipts** | 40% | Client results, before/afters, 90-day case studies, real training-log data |
| **Fat-loss myths (busted with science)** | 30% | Myths Dan's clients believe, corrected with the actual mechanism |
| **The coaching process** | 20% | How the coaching actually works — protocols, screenshots of plans, mistakes fixed |
| **Behind the scenes** | 10% | Training, coaching calls, the reality of running an online coaching business |

---

## Hook bank (≥ 8 — every hook is one of the 4 patterns)

### Pattern A — Specific outcome
- "Day 1 vs Day 90: the difference wasn't the workout. It was this."
- "This one training variable moved more fat in 90 days than any diet I've seen."

### Pattern B — Contrarian claim
- "You don't need to 'eat less' — you need to eat the right amount of the right things. Here's the math."
- "Your metabolism isn't broken. Your routine is. Fix this one thing first."
- "Cardio burns calories. Strength training changes what your body does with them."

### Pattern C — Open loop
- "I've coached 40 busy professionals this year — and 38 of them made the same 3 mistakes."
- "Nobody talks about this: stress is quietly undoing your fat loss."
- "What I tell clients who 'can't lose weight' — straight talk, no nonsense."

### Pattern D — Pattern interrupt / utility
- "The 20-minute workout that beats an hour at the gym (science, not vibes)."
- "The 5-minute habit that fixes your protein intake (screenshot this)."

## Angle bank (2–3 fresh angles per pillar — so 30 days don't repeat)

### Transformation receipts
1. The 90-day case study of one client (data: weight, strength, sleep, adherence).
2. "This client didn't change her workout — we changed her week." (schedule redesign)
3. Before/after photo with the honest timeline + what actually changed.

### Fat-loss myths
1. "Eating less" vs "eating the right amount" — the protein math.
2. The metabolism myth — what's actually slow (routine, sleep, stress).
3. Cardio vs strength — what each actually does to body composition.

### The coaching process
1. The exact 20-minute workout protocol (exercise, sets, RPE, rest).
2. A coaching screenshot with the fix explained (redacted).
3. The 3 mistakes framework — mistake → consequence → fix, one post per mistake.

### Behind the scenes
1. A coaching call debrief (what the client said, what Dan changed).
2. Dan's own training week (walk-through, sets, progression).
3. The business reality — how coaching days actually look.

## CTA rules (one per post, from the bank in plan.json)

- Save-bait for utility content ("Save this for your next training week").
- Comment-gated for frameworks ("Comment 'PROTOCOL' and I'll send the framework").
- Follow for series ("Follow for the day-by-day breakdown").
- Share for receipts ("Share this with someone who's stuck").
- DM for offers ("DM me 'START' if you want the 30-day plan").

Never: engagement-bait CTAs, fake vulnerability, or like-bait — all punished or
down-weighted on every platform.
