# Strategy — Algorithm Reset for Dan (fitness coach)

**The honest framing (read this first):** there's no magic switch that resets an
algorithm, and no promised view counts. The reset is a **14-day re-training
sprint**:
consistent cadence, a narrow niche cluster, and first-hour engagement velocity —
so the recommendation model re-learns who to show this account to. Views grow
in tiers, and the metrics loop (metrics.md) doubles down on whatever the data
says wins. Everything below ties to real ranking signals, not hacks.

- **Niche cluster (locked for the whole sprint):** fat-loss + strength training
  for busy professionals 30–45. Every post, every week, no side topics.
- **Proof arsenal:** 40 coached clients this year · 38 made the same 3 mistakes ·
  client before/afters (with permission) · 90-day case studies · RPE + protein
  data from real training logs.
- **Tone:** straight talk, no nonsense, science-backed but never clinical.
  Never: "detox", "miracle", "overnight", "shred".

---

## Instagram

**How it ranks in 2026:** four separate systems (Feed, Reels, Stories,
Explore) over Meta's five shared signals: relationships, interest, relevancy,
popularity, usage. The model matches semantic embedding, not hashtags — so
niche consistency teaches it exactly who to show Dan to. Engagement beats
follower count; **shares (especially DM shares) are the top Reels signal**;
watch time, rewatches, and saves decide distribution in the first 24–48h.

**The reset sprint for Instagram (days 1–14):**
- 5 posts/week: 3 Reels + 1 carousel + 1 static (Reels majority — it's the
  discovery surface).
- Every post inside the fat-loss-for-busy-professionals cluster. Same
  vocabulary every week ("protein", "strength", "busy schedule", "90 days").
- **First 60 minutes after each post:** reply to every comment, answer every
  DM, comment with ONE insight on 5–10 fitness/busy-professional accounts.
- Use **Trial Reels** for 1 Reel/week — test the hook on a cold audience
  before committing it to followers.
- Never delete underperformers (deleting removes training signal). Never
  repost TikTok watermarks (down-ranked).

**Formats that win:** Reels < 60s with a hook in the first 1.5s and a looped
ending · captions on · original audio or trending audio with original angle ·
carousels (5 slides: claim → proof → process → data → save-bait) · static
posts only for receipts with a story.

**Cadence + best time:** 5 posts/week at 7:30 AM ET (Insights says Dan's
audience is online mid-morning) + 3–5 Stories/week (1–5 slides, poll or
question sticker to spark replies).

**Hashtags / keywords:** 3–5 relevant tags (`#fatlossforbusyprofessionals`,
`#strengthtraining`, `#protein`, `#fitover30`) plus keywords in the caption
itself — the model reads text. Simple, translatable language widens reach via
AI translations.

**Pitfalls to avoid:** engagement pods (suppressed) · reply-bait CTAs ·
posting the same format 5× (the model narrows you) · generic "fitness tips"
posts that break the cluster.

## X

**How it ranks in 2026:** interest-graph "For You" feed where **first-hour
engagement velocity decides tier expansion**. Likes are baseline; replies,
quote-posts, and dwell time (how long people read) carry the weight. Niche
authority comes from consistency — the model clusters Dan's account by what
people who engage with him also read.

**The reset sprint for X (days 1–14):**
- 7 posts/week: 5 short posts + 1 numbered thread + 1 quote-post.
- After every post, spend the first 60 minutes replying to every reply and
  quoting 1–2 niche accounts with added insight (never an empty repost).
- Keep underperformers public. Reply in the vocabulary of busy professionals
  ("lose fat", "strength", "protein", "no time to train").

**Formats that win:** short posts under ~240 chars, one idea each · numbered
threads (one idea per post, open loop across the thread — e.g. the 3-mistakes
framework) · quote-posts of fitness/health accounts with Dan's take · native
video < 60s for quick form demos.

**Cadence + best time:** 7 posts/week (roughly 1/day) at 8:00 AM ET — morning
scroll window for professionals.

**Hashtags / keywords:** 0–2 tags max; the model reads the text, so write the
keywords ("protein", "strength", "fat loss", "busy") into the body.

**Pitfalls to avoid:** reply-bait farming ("unpopular opinion:" with nothing
behind it — throttled) · follow-for-follow · deleting underperformers · a
15-post thread that splits one idea (one idea per post).

## LinkedIn

**How it ranks in 2026:** the most follow-graph-weighted major platform, now a
hybrid — **dwell time and first-hour comment velocity drive distribution**
beyond Dan's network. Posts that hold a reader 5+ seconds get exponentially
more reach than scrolled-past posts with similar likes. First-hour comments
(from 1st-degree connections especially) push posts into 2nd-degree networks:
30 substantive comments in the first hour beat 300 likes over 24h. PDF
carousels are the highest-dwell format in 2026.

**The reset sprint for LinkedIn (days 1–14):**
- 3 posts/week (no more — LinkedIn punishes broadcast spam): 1 PDF carousel +
  1 text post + 1 native video.
- Turn on **Creator Mode**. After each post: 30–60 min commenting on 5–10
  targets (busy-professional peers, HR/fitness-industry accounts, Dan's 3
  competitors) with ONE added insight — never "Great post!".
- First line of every post is the hook; the post must hold a 5-second read.

**Formats that win:** PDF carousels — 8 slides of scroll-worthy value (the
3-mistakes framework, a 90-day case study, the 20-minute workout protocol) —
20+ seconds of dwell each · text posts with a story or contrarian take
("Your metabolism isn't broken. Your routine is.") · native vertical video.

**Cadence + best time:** 3 posts/week, Tue–Thu, 9:00 AM ET.

**Hashtags / keywords:** 2–3 niche tags max (`#fitnesscoach`, `#strengthtraining`,
`#fatloss`) — the feed reads text; long hashtag walls hurt dwell.

**Pitfalls to avoid:** engagement-bait ("Agree? 👇" — punished) · article-link
posts (links suppressed — quote the insight instead) · polls as filler
(deprioritized) · corporate template-speak (kills dwell on the first line).

## YouTube (Shorts + long-form)

**How it ranks in 2026:** two separate systems that must be designed for
independently. The **Shorts feed** ranks on completion + swipe velocity
(snackable discovery); **long-form** ranks on total session time, gated by CTR
(4–8% vs the topic cluster) and the retention curve — intro retention ≥ ~70%
at 30s, dips mark sections to cut, scrub-back moments are expandable gold.
Shorts and long-form have separate audience models: Shorts subscribers rarely
watch long-form unless you explicitly bridge the two.

**The reset sprint for YouTube (days 1–14):**
- 4 uploads/week: 3 Shorts + 1 long-form (8–12 min), every one inside the
  fat-loss-for-busy-professionals cluster — YouTube clusters by CHANNEL topic,
  so one niche across both systems.
- Shorts: first 1s hook, loop the ending. Long-form: the intro must clear the
  ~70%-at-30-seconds bar before anything else — open a loop in the first 30s.
- Never delete underperformers; never upload watermarked cross-posts (throttled).
- Build the **Shorts→long-form bridge**: one Short per week ends with a real
  hook for the full video ("the full 20-minute protocol is on the channel") —
  not a link-only caption.

**Formats that win:** Shorts 9:16 < 60s · long-form with a designed retention
curve (hook < 30s → 3 chapters → payoff map) · community posts (polls to keep
the test audience warm between uploads) · the weekly bridge Short.

**Cadence + best time:** 4 uploads/week on a fixed day/time (6:00 PM ET) — the
audience model learns the upload rhythm.

**Hashtags / keywords:** tags are minor — title keywords + the first 30 seconds
carry search intent. Title formula: specific outcome ("I got a client from 25%
to 18% body fat in 90 days — the exact plan"), not clickbait.

**Pitfalls to avoid:** CTR below 4–8% (throttled regardless of quality) · an
intro that loses 30%+ of viewers in 30 seconds · Shorts and long-form on
different topics (two audiences, no bridge) · deleting underperformers.

## The 14-day non-negotiables (all platforms)

1. **Same cluster, every post** — one niche, one vocabulary, 14 days straight.
2. **First-60-minute protocol after every post** (see engagement.md).
3. **Native or nothing** — adapt formats per platform; no watermarked cross-posts.
4. **No deleting, no pods, no bait** — every post is training signal.
5. **Log the 4 signals daily** (metrics.md) — Day 7 / 14 reviews adjust the plan.
