# The Algorithm "Reset" — what it actually is (read this FIRST)

## The honest truth

**There is no reset button and no guaranteed view count.** Nobody can promise
"1,000 views by Friday" — anyone who does is selling something. What you CAN
do is engineer the conditions every 2025–26 algorithm rewards, and let the
recommendation model re-learn your account. That is the reset.

## Why a stalled account stays stalled

All major platforms now rank with **interest-graph recommendation models**
(Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads). The model
embeds your content into a topic vector space and shows it to users whose
history matches that vector. A stalled account has one of two problems:

1. **A weak embedding** — erratic posting (3 posts, 10-day gap), mixed topics
   (finance Monday, memes Tuesday, fitness Friday), or recycled cross-posts
   mean the model can't tell who you're for, so it tests you on nobody.
2. **Drowned signal** — low completion, skipped posts, zero first-hour
   engagement teach the model your content is low-quality, so it stops
   testing you with new audiences.

## The reset = a 14-day re-training sprint

Give the model **dense, consistent, unambiguous signals** for 14 days and it
re-tests your account with fresh audiences. The sprint has five parts:

| Part | What you do | Signal it sends |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cadence lock** | Post on a fixed schedule (per platform playbook) — never 5 posts then a week of silence | Consistency → stable embedding |
| **2. Niche clustering** | Every post lives inside 2–4 narrow pillars; same vocabulary every week | The model learns exactly who to show you to |
| **3. First-60-minute velocity** | After each post: reply to every comment, comment with ONE insight on 5–10 niche accounts | Early engagement trains the retrieval tier → second-tier distribution |
| **4. Completion/share engineering** | Hooks in the first 1.5–3s (video) / first 2 lines (text), loop endings, save/share triggers | Watch time, completion, shares = the heavy signals |
| **5. Never delete underperformers** | Keep them public; read the retention data instead | Every post is training signal; deletions starve the model |

## How views actually grow

In **tiers**, not spikes: a post clears its first test cohort → wider test →
feed/Explore/For You distribution. Small accounts win by letting the model
learn *one* thing about them really well, then expanding niche-to-niche into
adjacent audiences. The sprint's job is to get re-tested; the 30-day
calendar's job is to keep the signal dense after the re-test.

## The rules that make it honest (also the quality bar)

- **Native per platform.** Cross-posting with watermarks, wrong aspect ratios,
  or recycled formats triggers distribution penalties on every platform.
- **No engagement-bait.** "Agree? 👇", "Who else feels this?", fake
  vulnerability — punished or down-weighted everywhere.
- **No guarantees.** The plan promises a process, a cadence, and a feedback
  loop — not a view count.
- **Data, not vibes.** Day 7 / 14 / 21 / 30 reviews adjust the plan on
  dwell, completion, save/share, and follower-conversion numbers.

> **The one-line version to put in every strategy.md:**
> "The reset is a 14-day re-training sprint: consistent cadence, a narrow
> niche cluster, and first-hour engagement velocity — so the recommendation
> model re-learns who to show this account to. Views grow in tiers, and the
> metrics loop doubles down on whatever the data says wins."
