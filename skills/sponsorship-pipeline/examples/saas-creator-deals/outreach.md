## Negotiation notes (from templates/outreach-scripts.md)

- **Anchor high:** when asked "what do you charge," quote the rate card's high end and let the range give room.
- **Bundle to close:** never discount the flagship video — add the newsletter mention + LinkedIn post instead (deal size up, flagship rate intact).
- **Cadence:** day 3 bump → day 7 new proof point → day 14 park with "ping me when Q3 campaigns open." No spam.
- **Scope creep:** extra placements, deliverables, or usage rights are line items. Nothing free.
- **FTC:** every sponsored post ships with #ad / #sponsored in the first lines — tell the sponsor this up front; it's a feature.
