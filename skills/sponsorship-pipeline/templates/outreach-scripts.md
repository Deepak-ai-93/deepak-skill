# Outreach + negotiation — template for sponsorship-pipeline

Every pitch is a micro-story: **the sponsor's problem (open loop) → your proof (rising tension) → the offer (payoff) → the ask (loop ending)**. Never a template — a pitch you could send to any brand is a pitch you should not send at all.

## The pitch structure (every pitch has all four)

```
### Pitch {N} — {sponsor name}
**Problem:** {their campaign need / audience gap — name their product + their current campaign}
**Proof:** {your audience stats + engagement that match their need — the specific numbers}
**Offer:** {the format + placement + deliverable + rate, from the rate card}
**Ask:** {one next step — a 15-min call / a test run / "can I send the media kit?"}
**Disclosure:** {#ad / #sponsored placement — every sponsored post is labeled}
```

## Cold pitch (first contact)

- Subject: `{their campaign} × {your niche} — a fit?` (specific, short)
- 100–150 words. Their problem in the first sentence. One proof number. One offer. One ask.
- Attach or link the media kit — but the email itself must stand alone.

## Negotiation scripts

- **Price anchoring:** quote the rate card's high end first when asked "what do you charge" — the range gives room; never quote a single number with no room.
- **Bundling:** "The dedicated video is $X, but the bundle (video + newsletter mention + IG story) is $Y — the campaign reaches three audiences." Bundles raise the deal size without discounting the flagship.
- **The no-answer follow-up cadence:** day 3 → "bumping this to the top of your inbox"; day 7 → one new proof point (a fresh result); day 14 → "happy to park this — ping me when the Q{next} campaigns open"; then park. Never spam.
- **Scope creep:** every add-on (extra placement, extra deliverable, usage rights) is a line item. Nothing free.
- **The "too expensive" reply:** ask what the budget is; if it's below your floor, offer the trimmed bundle or a usage-rights carve — never discount your flagship.

## FTC disclosure (non-negotiable)

- Every sponsored post/mention carries **#ad or #sponsored in the FIRST lines** of the post (before the fold — no "link in comments", no buried tags).
- The pitch itself tells the sponsor this is how you ship — disclosure is a selling point, not a concession.
- Platforms: Instagram/Facebook → #ad; YouTube → "Includes paid promotion" toggle + verbal disclosure; TikTok/X → #ad; newsletter → "Sponsored by" header.
