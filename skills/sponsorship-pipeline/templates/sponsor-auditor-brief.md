## The standards you're enforcing

- The sponsor's problem leads the kit + pitches (audience fit, not the resume).
- Honest numbers: engagement over followers, benchmark-grounded rate ranges, no guarantees, no fabricated results.
- Personalized outreach: every pitch names the sponsor's product/campaign + the specific fit; zero placeholders.
- FTC disclosure (#ad / #sponsored) in every pitch + deliverable template.
- Tracking: pipeline columns + a row per outreach + the day 3/7/14 follow-up cadence.
