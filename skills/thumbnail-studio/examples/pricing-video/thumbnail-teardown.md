# Thumbnail teardown — SaaS pricing niche

**Niche:** SaaS / B2B founders · **The ONE idea:** the mistake that quietly costs founders $12k — and the one-line fix.

## What the niche's winners do (last ~3 months)

- **The realization face** — founder mid-thought, warm desk lamp, screen glow. The emotion sells, the screen shows the outcome. ~4/10 top thumbnails.
- **Split / comparison** — wrong way vs right way, often with a hand or arrow pointing at the right side. ~3/10.
- **The number reveal** — a close-up of a metric, dashboard, or price tag; the number IS the hook. ~3/10.

## What's saturated

- The generic shocked-face-with-hands reaction (niche is past it).
- Red arrows + circles on screenshots — everyone does it, no one trusts it.
- "GONE WRONG" impact-font thumbnails (burned out in this niche).

## The pattern we break

All three winners above are fine as *executions* — the break is in the **promise**: most pricing-niche thumbnails tease "mistakes" generically. Ours names the cost ("costs $12k") in the overlay, so the curiosity gap is specific: *which* mistake, *how* does it cost that much, and what's the one-line fix. Specific beats generic, always.

## Why these 3 variants

1. **Realization face + named cost** — the emotional rail, with the specific $ in the overlay.
2. **Split wrong-way vs right-way** — the comparison rail, directly actionable.
3. **Price-tag close-up** — the number rail, for feed positions where the face reads small.

The A/B pair (variants 1 + 2) are the most *different* executions: emotion-led vs structure-led — exactly what the test should compare.
