# Thumbnail rules — reference for thumbnail-studio

The thumbnail is the hook of the hook: it makes a scroller STOP, then the title + first frame make them CLICK, then the video pays off. If the thumbnail promises what the video can't pay off, the click dies in the first 30 seconds.

## The ONE-idea rule (non-negotiable)

Each thumbnail sells **exactly ONE idea** — pick the formula, then stop:

| Formula | What it looks like | Overlay example |
|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity gap** | An unresolved question, a forbidden reveal, a "why does nobody…" | "why nobody does this" |
| **Result / transformation** | The outcome, the number, the before→after | "$12k in 30 days" |
| **Threat / loss aversion** | The mistake, the cost, what you're losing | "the mistake that costs $12k" |
| **Comparison / vs** | Two sides, the wrong way vs the right way | "your way vs mine" |
| **List / countdown** | A number + the promise | "5 pricing mistakes" |

Never mash two formulas. If the idea needs a sentence, cut the qualifiers.

## The readability rails

- **Face + emotion** — a real emotion on a real face (or a high-tension object). Emotion reads faster than words at 120×68px.
- **Contrast** — subject vs background, one accent color. The thumbnail must survive a thumbnail-sized render.
- **Overlay ≤ 5 words**, ONE font family, on a high-contrast band/scrim. Text adds the promise; the image carries the emotion.
- **Composition** — rule of thirds, subject off-center, headroom for the overlay, nothing critical in the corner badges (duration / progress bar zone).

## The anti-cliché blocklist

No red arrows · no red circles · no hands-on-cheeks shock · no "YOU WON'T BELIEVE" · no "GONE WRONG" in impact font · no "$" raining · no rocket ships · no clip-art explosions · no "click here" / "watch this" · no generic stock "strategy" icons as the main visual.

If everyone in the niche does the shocked face, do the opposite — the teardown decides.

## Niche saturation check

Before generating: what do the top 5–10 thumbnails in the niche ALL do? If 8 of 10 use the same composition, that pattern is saturated — the winning variant is the one that *breaks* it while keeping the same promise.

## Canvas + text

- Generate at **1280×720** (YouTube) — the standard.
- Overlay text on the generated image may garble — verify every word, and if it's wrong, regenerate or do a text overlay pass in an editor. The plan's `overlay` is the exact string to render.
