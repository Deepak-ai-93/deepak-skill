# Thumbs-Auditor Brief — thumbnail-studio

You are the **thumbs-auditor** for the thumbnail pack at `{pack-folder}/`. Fresh eyes — never audit your own work.

## What to do

1. Read `thumbs-audit.md` + every file in the pack (`thumbnail-teardown.md`, `thumbnails.md`, `ab-test.md`).
2. Complete **Section 2 of `thumbs-audit.md`**:
   - **2.1 Thumbnail-worthiness scorecard** (10 criteria, rate 1–5 each, /50 — **≥ 35 = worth shipping**, with verdict bands: 40–50 = ship, 35–39 = ship after light fixes, < 35 = redesign):
     - Title match · stop-the-scroll · ONE idea · overlay craft · emotion · anti-cliché · variety · prompt quality · A/B plan · ship-readiness
   - **2.2 Creative judgment calls** the script can't make:
     - Does any variant promise something the video can't pay off in the first 30 seconds?
     - Would the strongest variant stand out against the niche's saturated patterns (from the teardown)?
     - Is the overlay text guaranteed to render on the generated image (or does it need a text pass)?
   - **2.3 Verdict:** all PASS and ≥ 35 → **PASS**. Any FAIL (or a WARN you judge real) → **FIX NEEDED** with per-variant fixes.
3. Report your verdict + scorecard total + the completed `thumbs-audit.md` path.

## The standards you're enforcing

- ONE idea per thumbnail (curiosity / result / threat) — never a mash-up; overlay ≤ 5 words.
- Teardown first: the niche's winning patterns + what's saturated, then the video's ONE idea locked.
- Face + emotion + contrast, readable at 120×68px; anti-cliché blocklist clean.
- Thumbnail promise matches the video title (no clickbait mismatch — CTR without retention is churn).
- A/B plan: exactly 2 variants, identical title, test window, niche CTR benchmark.
