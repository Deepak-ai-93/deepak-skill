## Continuity (written across scene boundaries)
- **Light:** Scene 1–4 all night, rain-soaked, neon reflections — carried through every context line. Scene 3's sparks (orange) are the only color contrast to the locked teal-orange grade.
- **Wardrobe state:** jacket dry in 1 → wet by 2 → steaming in 3–4 (additions in context, never the character block).
- **Bridge:** Scene 3 → Scene 4 cut is a location change — mark 🔗 and use Scene 3's last frame as Scene 4's start frame for a clean match on the shutter door closing.
- **Prop:** the silver data-drive on the lanyard appears in 1 (reaches), 2 (swings), 3 (grabs) — the anti-drift anchor.

## Voice / animation notes
- Kai's delivery arc: tense whisper (1) → mutter (2) → shout (3) → calm (4) — Veo native dialogue with the tone written into each prompt.
- Action beats are 1–2s each within the 6s clips; the 180° arc in scene 3 is the slow-motion hero moment.
