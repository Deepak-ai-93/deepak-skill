# X Growth Plan — solo SaaS pricing + positioning for indie founders

**Goal:** Grow from 2,000 to 4,000 followers in 30 days — replies and quote posts are the leading indicators, followers the lagging one
**Audience:** Indie SaaS founders building alone (0–3 employees), pre-revenue to $10k MRR · **Voice:** Direct, first-person founder-to-founder. Short sentences, real numbers, no marketing speak, admits mistakes · **Cadence:** 1 post(s)/day
**Pillars:** Pricing lessons · Positioning & messaging · Building in public

## The growth rails (every post in this plan follows them)

- **Top-creator post format.** Every post is a 500–800 char micro-essay: a hook line ≤ 100 chars that opens a loop → story/context → bullet points → payoff → CTA. Line breaks every 1–2 sentences — never a wall of text.
- **Zero hashtags.** No tags anywhere — X growth is copy-first, hashtags are noise and read as spam. The script FAILS any post with a `#`.
- **One micro-story per post.** The first line opens the loop, the last line closes it — every post is complete alone and scannable in 20 seconds.
- **The week is the series.** Hook → value → proof/story → CTA/loop across the week — serialization makes the account worth following, not one viral post.
- **Fluff rule:** if a post can be deleted without losing the story, delete it.

## Engagement schedule (the posts earn reach; the replies compound it)

- **Reply-first 60 minutes** after each post: reply to every reply, in order, within minutes — a reply trail under a post gets it pushed again.
- **Quote posts:** 3–5 per day — quote the niche's big accounts with ONE sharp take (never 'Great post!'), never more than one line of your take + link.
- **DM follow-ups:** match the CTA of the day — answer DMs within 24h, qualify with one question, never mass-DM the same text.
- **Follow hygiene:** follow back genuine engagement same day, 20–40 follows/day max from real niche searches, prune spam weekly.
