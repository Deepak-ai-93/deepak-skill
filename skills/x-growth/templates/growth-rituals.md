# Growth rituals — reference for x-growth

The posts earn reach; the replies compound it. Every growth pack ships a content plan with an engagement schedule built from these rituals. **Zero hashtags everywhere — including replies.**

## The reply-first hour (non-negotiable)

The first 60 minutes after a post decide whether the algorithm pushes it:

1. **Post at your niche's peak window** (check when your last 10 posts got replies — usually 7–9am or 6–8pm local).
2. **Stay online for the full hour.** Reply to EVERY reply, in order, within minutes — a post with a reply trail under it gets pushed again.
3. **Reply with substance, not emojis.** One line that extends the post ("The wild part: it cost us $4k to learn"). Every reply is a new beat of the story.
4. **Quote-reply the good replies** — a strong take from a reader becomes the day's bonus post ("This — and it gets worse…").
5. **Don't reply to hate with hate.** A short, self-aware reply ("fair — but the data says…") turns a hater into engagement.

## The quote-post strategy (daily compounding)

3–5 quote posts a day keep the account alive between original posts:

- **Quote the niche's big accounts** with ONE sharp take (never "Great post!" — that's noise).
- **Quote your own old posts** that are still getting likes — one line of new insight.
- **Quote news in the niche** with a contrarian angle — news posts get search + engagement.
- Format: one line of your take + the link. No hashtags.

## DM follow-ups (match the CTA)

- If the day's CTA was "DM me X": reply to every DM within 24h; qualify with one question ("what's your current setup?"), not a pitch.
- If the CTA was a link: reply with the link only when asked.
- Never mass-DM the same text — that's the fastest way to a shadowban.

## Follow/unfollow hygiene

- Follow back genuine engagement (repliers, sharers) same day.
- Don't follow-bot — 20–40 follows/day max, from real search results in the niche.
- Prune spam followers monthly; keep the follower:following ratio sane.

## The weekly series loop (the growth engine)

Fast growth on X is a **series, not a single hit**:

1. **Plan the week** (this skill) — hook → value → story/proof → cta/loop mix, one post per pillar.
2. **Post daily** at the peak window, reply-first hour each time.
3. **Day-7 review** (below) — kill what's flat, double what works.
4. **Pivot the next week** — the weak pillar gets a new hook formula; the strong pillar becomes the CTA post.

## Metrics to watch (weekly, day-7 review)

| Metric | Healthy signal | Fix if weak |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions per post | Rising week over week | Hook is weak → rewrite the first line |
| Reply rate (replies/impressions) | > 0.3% | Ask a real question; take a stronger position |
| Quote posts (shares) | > 1% | The take isn't quotable — sharpen the payoff |
| Profile visits | Posts → bio → follow | CTA post is weak or the bio doesn't match |
| Follower conversion | > 2% of impressions | CTA post + pinned post + bio alignment |
