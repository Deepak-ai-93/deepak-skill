# Engagement plan — "5 pricing mistakes that killed my last SaaS"

## The reply-first hour (post at 8am CET, stay for 60 min)

1. Reply to every reply in order, within minutes — each reply extends the thread ("The wild part: #3 alone cost us ~$3k in missed trials").
2. Quote-reply the strongest takes — a reader's pricing story becomes tweet 9 of the thread ("This is #6 — comment it and I'll include it in the teardown").
3. Stay off the DM inbox during the hour; DMs come after.

## Quote-post strategy (3–5/day around the thread)

- Quote 2 pricing/founder posts from the niche with a one-line take each day.
- Quote this thread itself in 2 days: "The pricing page teardown is coming — the numbers surprised me."
- Never "Great post!" — one sharp line or nothing.

## DM follow-ups

- CTA is "follow for part 2" → no DMs needed; reply to anyone who asks "when's part 2?" with a date.
- If someone DMs a pricing question → answer + one clarifying question, no pitch.

## Follow hygiene

- Follow back every replier same day (20–40/day max).
- Prune spam accounts weekly.

## Day-7 review

| Metric | Target | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | rising vs last thread | — |
| Reply rate | > 0.3% | — |
| Quote posts | > 1% | — |

If reply rate is flat → the hook needs a sharper open loop. If quote posts are flat → the payoff needs to be more quotable. If followers didn't move → the CTA tweet + pinned thread + bio don't line up.
