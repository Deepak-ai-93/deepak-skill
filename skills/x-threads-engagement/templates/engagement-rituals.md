# Engagement rituals — reference for x-threads-engagement

The thread gets the reach; the replies compound it. Every thread pack ships an `engagement.md` built from these rituals.

## The reply-first hour (non-negotiable)

The first 60 minutes after posting decide whether the algorithm pushes the thread:

1. **Post at your niche's peak window** (check when your last 10 threads got replies — usually 7–9am or 6–8pm local).
2. **Stay online for the full hour.** Reply to EVERY reply, in order, within minutes — a thread with a reply trail under it gets pushed again.
3. **Reply with substance, not emojis.** One line that extends the thread ("The wild part: it cost us $4k to learn"). Every reply is a new beat of the story.
4. **Quote-reply the good replies** — a strong take from a reader becomes tweet N+1 of the thread ("This — and it gets worse…").
5. **Don't reply to hate with hate.** A short, self-aware reply ("fair — but the data says…") turns a hater into engagement.

## The quote-post strategy (daily compounding)

Between threads, 3–5 quote posts a day keep the account alive:

- **Quote the niche's big accounts** with ONE sharp take (never "Great post!" — that's noise).
- **Quote your own old threads** that are still getting likes — one line of new insight.
- **Quote news in the niche** with a contrarian angle — news threads get search + engagement.
- Format: one line of your take + the link + no more than 3 hashtags.

## DM follow-ups (match the CTA)

- If the CTA was "DM me X": reply to every DM within 24h; qualify with one question ("what's your current setup?"), not a pitch.
- If the CTA was a link: reply to thread replies with the link only when asked.
- Never mass-DM the same text — that's the fastest way to a shadowban.

## Follow/unfollow hygiene

- Follow back genuine engagement (repliers, sharers) same day.
- Don't follow-bot — 20–40 follows/day max, from real search results in the niche.
- Prune spam followers monthly; keep the follower:following ratio sane.

## Metrics to watch (weekly, day-7 review)

| Metric | Healthy signal | Fix if weak |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions per thread | Rising week over week | Hook is weak → rewrite tweet 1 |
| Reply rate (replies/impressions) | > 0.3% | Ask a real question; take a stronger position |
| Quote posts (shares) | > 1% | The take isn't quotable — sharpen the payoff |
| Profile visits | Threads → bio → follow | CTA tweet is weak or the bio doesn't match |
| Follower conversion | > 2% of impressions | CTA + bio + pinned thread alignment |
